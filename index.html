FOTSPOT.UZ

A platform that helps grassroots football players in Uzbekistan get discovered. Players build a professional profile backed by video evidence, scouts recommend talent, and academies search by age, position and region, invite players to closed trials and manage squads in one system. Players can also apply directly to open trials. Profiles of players under 18 are private by default, with no private messaging between adults and children. Technologies: Next.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL
